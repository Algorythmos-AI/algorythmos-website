## Map data

The globe in the 3D scenes places its continents from a land mask
(`src/lib/scene3d/kit/land.ts`, 180 × 90 cells) generated by
`scripts/build-land-mask.mjs` from Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons — public
domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com — as packaged by the `world-atlas` npm
module (dev dependency only; nothing from it ships).
